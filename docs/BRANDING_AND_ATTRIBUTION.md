# Branding and Attribution

Quantum Foundry is an independent personal project and is not an official Google product.

## Required Statement

Independent personal project. Not an official Google product. This project is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or maintained by Google LLC. It uses publicly available Google Cloud and Google Quantum AI ecosystem technologies where applicable.

## Product Name

Preferred public name: **Quantum Foundry**.

Avoid:

- GCP Quantum Foundry
- Google Quantum Foundry
- Google-native
- Google-built
- powered by Google
- official Google or official GCP
- endorsed by Google or sponsored by Google

Preferred descriptive phrasing:

- built with Google Cloud technologies
- uses Google Cloud services
- uses Cirq-based simulations
- uses publicly available Google Quantum AI ecosystem tools
- maps workflows to Google Cloud architecture patterns

## Trademark Notice

Google, Google Cloud, GCP, Vertex AI, Gemini, Cirq, qsim, OpenFermion, and related names are trademarks or products of their respective owners. References are descriptive.
